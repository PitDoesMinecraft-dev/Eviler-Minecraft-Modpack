THIS MODPACK CONTAINS A MODIFIED VERSION OF HARDCORE ENDER EXPANSION. IF YOU HAVE ANY ISSUES, PLEASE CONTACT ME AS I PROBALY WAS THAT ONE THAT BROKE SOMETHING, NOT CHYLEX.

Changes made from the original:

HARDCORE ENDER EXPANSION boss fight has made harder by adding back the bomb towers from an old Hardcore Ender dragon 1.6 build, which now gives bad potion effects when near it... alongside a new pinch mode for the ender dragon. This makes him attack faster but also gain an additional new finishing move. 

In addition, the ender daemon boss has been made fightable. The way the summon it is not like the original way and is a W.I.P and very buggy(sorry)

The music Ticker has the awkward delay fixed when fighting the dragon.

When bloodlust attack is used, a max of 1 damage can be taken.

Normal ender dragon spawns in alongside hardcore ender dragoon in flight.

Hardcore ender dragon always returns worst effects when bitten.

The "YOUR DOOM" and "DERPY LIZZARD" texts was added back to be shown in the boss bar from the old Hardcore Ender Dragon.

The dragon will now spawn in silverfish when fireball hits.

Ender daemon structure added back(is buggy right now, my fault... Sorry). Ender apocalypse is also missing as well...
